UIL Math Trainer

Welcome to UIL Math Trainer

This site is designed to help students prepare for UIL Mathematics through organized practice, structured test simulations, and access to past contest material. Use the tabs above to navigate between practice problems, full practice tests, and archived UIL exams.

Practice

This section will contain topic‑based practice problems. You’ll be able to choose specific categories and work through problems one at a time.

Practice Tests

This section will offer full‑length practice tests modeled after real UIL exams. You’ll be able to take timed or untimed tests and review your results.

Past Tests

This section will include a collection of previous UIL Mathematics tests. You’ll be able to browse by year and access official contest materials.
